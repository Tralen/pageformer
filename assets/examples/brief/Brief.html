Orbital Weather Brief

This brief summarizes the spring observing window for the Northwind Observatory. It collects the instrument readiness review, the method used to combine the atmospheric models and the schedule for the next campaign.

Method

The outlook combines the station readings with the regional model. The steps are the same for every campaign:

  1. Collect the hourly station records;

    1. Read the pressure and humidity channels;

    2. Reject the samples flagged by the quality check;

  2. Merge the regional model;

  3. Publish the outlook.

The merge is a small, deterministic function:

def merge(station, model, weight=0.6):
    return [weight * s + (1 - weight) * m for s, m in zip(station, model)]

The weight favors the station because its sensors sit closer to the ground. The model fills the gaps between launches.

A short quote marks the decision: the outlook is published only after the station lead reviews the merged series.
